Apple Screen Time

This app only changes Firewalla. Beck's iPhone also uses cellular, so Apple Downtime and App Limits still apply.

Easiest: have Beck tap Ask For More Time on the blocked app, then approve 15 minutes or 1 hour to match. Apple's approvals expire on their own, so there's nothing to turn back on.

Firewalla blocks only cover Wi-Fi. On cellular, Beck's iPhone can still reach these apps.

To close that gap, turn off cellular data for those apps (iPhone Settings → Cellular), then lock it with Screen Time → Content & Privacy → Cellular Data Changes: Don't Allow.
